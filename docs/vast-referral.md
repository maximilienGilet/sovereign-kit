# Optional Vast.ai referral link

Vast.ai operates a referral program. Its official documentation states that a referral link earns **3% of a referred new client’s lifetime credit spend** as referral credits, with up to **75%** cash-out through the supported payout methods.

Source: [Vast.ai Referral Program](https://docs.vast.ai/guides/reference/referral-program) (consulted 2026-09-01).

## Important account rule

For cash payouts, Vast says to use a dedicated new referral account. An account that has rented instances or hosted machines cannot cash out until referral earnings exceed its lifetime instance spend. The regular operating account can still earn/use referral credits.

Do not self-refer or refer accounts connected to you; Vast says these do not earn rewards.

## Adding a link safely

1. Create the dedicated referral account yourself.
2. In Vast, open **Settings → Referral Link** and copy the generated URL.
3. Add it to the README only after you have the exact URL.
4. Include a plain disclosure next to it:

> **Referral disclosure:** this link is a Vast.ai referral link. If you create a new Vast account through it and purchase credits, I may receive referral credits or a commission at no additional cost to you.

A public template may also expose a template-specific referral link from **Templates → My Templates → three-dot menu → Copy Referral Link**. Do not include a template with credentials, private images, or client-specific environment variables.
